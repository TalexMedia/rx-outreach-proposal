01 The Conversation

What We Heard

Our synthesis of your audit call and the situation you're navigating.

When we spoke, the picture was clear. The testimonials are already arriving. A mother writing over the weekend about her daughter turning 26, losing her insurance, and being handed a $3,000-a-month medication, then crediting Rx Outreach with saving her daughter's life. The stories are real, organic, and frequent. What's missing is the bridge from a written review to a format a stranger will actually stop and watch.

You told us Katy is the entire marketing function today, that reviews come in steadily but converting them into shareable video feels like a new hurdle each time, and that your strongest patient stories are scattered nationally, not concentrated locally in St. Louis, so sourcing them affordably has been a real constraint. The sandwich-generation caregiver, the aging patient on the phone, the free and charitable clinic passing you on, these are three very different audiences asking for three different conversations.

You also told us a strategic plan is landing in the next few weeks, and that the audience clarity coming out of it will finally let you build around specific personas rather than "everyone nationally." This engagement is built to walk into that window, not around it.
